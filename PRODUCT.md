## Stack

## Users

Women entering or working in hairdressing, especially professionals seeking more confidence, consistency, clarity and professional value in highlights services.

## Product Purpose

Método Rose Oliveira is a digital learning product intended to help professionals develop their work from client service through finishing, with practical instruction focused on highlights.

## Positioning

Rose Oliveira shares the process behind the transformations she creates for clients so other professionals can develop their own technique and client experience. The public Hotmart listing says she accompanies students as a mentor during the journey.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The public Hotmart listing confirms client service through finishing, profitable highlights, simple practical teaching and mentorship by Rose. No module list, price, access duration, guarantee, certificate or quantified results has been provided. ## Product Principles

- Show the transformation passed from Rose to professionals to their clients.
- Speak to the professional's confidence and service value without promising earnings.
- Anchor factual claims in the public product description and supplied material.
- Make it easy to learn about the method and reach the official purchase destination.
